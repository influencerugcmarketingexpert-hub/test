## DESIGN NOTES

- **Color palette:** black, ivory/cream, deep cognac brown (leather tones)
- **Typography:** serif headlines (luxury feel) + sans-serif body
- **Hero:** must autoplay muted video, 8-12s loop, with poster image fallback
- **Animations:** subtle fade-up on scroll, image zoom on hover
- **Performance:** lazy-load all below-the-fold images
- **A11y:** all CTAs ≥ 44px tap target, alt text on all imagery
```
